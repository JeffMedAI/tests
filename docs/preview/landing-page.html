Data Security

Patient data never
leaves the building

Unlike cloud-based AI tools, Avamed runs entirely on hardware within the surgery premises. No patient information is sent to third-party servers, ever.

🔒
On-premises processing
Ollama runs locally on your server. Every AI inference happens inside the building — nothing sent to OpenAI, Azure, or any cloud.
📋
GDPR-ready by design
Automated 90-day data purge, full audit log in SQLite, and no patient data in version control.
🏥
NHS-aligned governance
DSPT, DTAC, and Cyber Essentials compliance in progress. DCB0129 clinical safety review underway.

NHS Digital Marketplace partnership
