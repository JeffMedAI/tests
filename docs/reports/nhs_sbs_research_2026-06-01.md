## B. DTAC — DIGITAL TECHNOLOGY ASSESSMENT CRITERIA

### What It Is
DTAC is the national baseline criteria for digital health technologies in the NHS and social care. Published by NHS England. Any technology used in the NHS should meet DTAC standards. It is self-assessed by the supplier and submitted to NHS organisations as evidence of baseline safety and compliance.

### Current Version
Updated form published April 2026 — 25% fewer questions than the previous version. Questions that duplicated DSPT requirements have been removed. Full transition to the new form required from 6 April 2026.

### Five Assessment Domains

| Domain | What It Requires | JeffLocal Position |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Clinical Safety | DCB0129 compliance, Clinical Safety Officer appointed, MHRA registration if applicable | **Action required: appoint CSO** |
| Data Protection | ICO registration, DSPT completion, data protection impact assessment | Strong — local architecture simplifies this |
| Technical Security | Pen testing (last 12 months), Cyber Essentials certification, fix priority vulnerabilities | **Action required: arrange pen test + Cyber Essentials** |
| Interoperability | Evidence of interoperability standards (HL7/FHIR) or documented roadmap | Document Phase 2 EMIS integration plan |
| Usability & Accessibility | Evidence of usability testing, WCAG compliance | Dashboard designed for receptionist use; document evidence |

### Key Actions for Avamed
1. **Appoint Clinical Safety Officer (CSO)** — can be Saeed initially; GP clinical advisor preferred for credibility
2. **Arrange penetration testing** — independent pen test of JeffLocal system (budget: £500–£3,000 for SME pen test)
3. **Achieve Cyber Essentials** — self-assessment, ~£300 assessment fee
4. **Register with ICO** — ~£40–60/year
5. **Complete DSPT** — see section C below

### Timeline
- DTAC self-assessment: 4–8 weeks for a well-prepared SME
- Can be submitted as "in progress" during framework application with a commitment statement

### Cost
No direct fee for the DTAC self-assessment itself. Associated costs:
- Cyber Essentials: ~£300 (self-assessment via approved certification body)
- Penetration testing: £500–£3,000 (independent provider)
- ICO registration: £40–60/year
- Clinical Safety Officer (if external): £500–£2,000 if contracting a GP/clinical advisor

## C. DSPT — DATA SECURITY AND PROTECTION TOOLKIT

### What It Is
The DSPT is an annual self-assessment covering 10 National Data Guardian standards. Required for any organisation that accesses NHS patient data or systems. Submission deadline is 30 June each year.

### Current Version
Version 8 (2025/26). Deadline: 30 June 2026.

### Avamed's Position
- As a technology supplier to an NHS GP surgery, Avamed needs to complete DSPT as part of demonstrating DTAC data protection compliance
- GP practices (Category 4) require ~42 mandatory evidence items — but as the technology supplier (not the practice itself), Avamed may fall under a different organisation type
- JeffLocal's **on-premise architecture is a significant advantage**: because no patient data leaves the surgery, the data protection scope is fundamentally simpler than for cloud-hosted systems
- The DSPT toolkit organises organisations by type at: https://www.dsptoolkit.nhs.uk/Help/5 — confirm the correct category for Avamed as a GP technology supplier

### Key Actions
1. Register at: https://www.dsptoolkit.nhs.uk/
2. Confirm organisation type / category
3. Begin self-assessment immediately — deadline 30 June 2026
4. Emphasise local data residency in evidence submissions

### Timeline
- Registration: immediate (1 day)
- Self-assessment completion: 2–4 weeks with focused effort

### Summary Table

| Competitor | Framework Status | Architecture | Monthly Price | Key Differentiator vs JeffLocal |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------|--------------|--------------------------------|
| Accurx | G-Cloud listed; NHS App integrated | Cloud-hosted SaaS | ~£442–£600+ | Used in 98% of GP practices; cloud data processing |
| eConsult | Multiple NHS frameworks | Cloud-hosted SaaS | ~£600–£892 | Online consultations (not telephone); cloud |
| Anima | NHS App integrated | Cloud-hosted SaaS | ~£500+ | AI-powered online consultations; cloud |
| JeffLocal | Applying SBS10523 | On-premise | £299–£449 | Only telephone-first, on-premise AI triage system |

### Key Insights

**All major competitors are cloud-hosted.** This is JeffLocal's structural differentiator. Accurx, eConsult, and Anima all transmit patient data to external cloud servers. JeffLocal keeps everything on-site. In the current NHS climate of heightened data protection scrutiny, this is a genuine competitive advantage — especially for practices concerned about cloud data flows under GDPR.

**Telephone vs. online channel.** The major competitors focus on online consultation (patients submitting requests via app or web form). JeffLocal is the only AI product in this market segment designed specifically for the telephone channel. This is not a niche — a large proportion of GP patients, particularly older and digitally excluded patients, cannot or do not use online consultation tools. JeffLocal serves these patients.

**Price positioning.** JeffLocal enters at £299/month, below the market floor of cloud competitors. This is possible because local AI has zero marginal cost per call — no cloud inference fees, no per-API-call charges.

**What competitors claimed to get listed.** Accurx and Anima achieved NHS framework listing and NHS App integration by demonstrating: (a) widespread adoption across GP practices as evidence of clinical utility, (b) DTAC completion, (c) DSPT compliance, and (d) integration with NHS systems. JeffLocal's Churchtown pilot relationship serves as the equivalent adoption evidence at this stage.
