## DAILY WHATSAPP BRIEFING

**Sent to:** 07440 333938 (Saeed's personal number — always verify before sending)
**When:** Automatically at 07:00 every day, AND on demand when Saeed requests it
**Who sends:** Strategy Agent prepares; DevOps Agent sends via scheduled task
**Format:** Plain English. No jargon. Use /caveman skill.

**Template:**
```
AVAMED DAILY BRIEFING — [Date]

YESTERDAY:
[2-4 bullets — what was completed, tested, and committed]

TODAY:
[2-3 bullets — what the team is working on today]

BLOCKERS:
[Anything stopping progress, or "None"]

NEEDS YOUR APPROVAL:
[List any decisions or sign-offs pending from Saeed, or "None"]
```

**Rules:**
- Maximum 200 words total
- No technical terms without a plain-English explanation in brackets
- If there is nothing to report, still send — "quiet day, no blockers" is better than silence
- If something urgent happened overnight, move it to the top with "URGENT:" prefix
